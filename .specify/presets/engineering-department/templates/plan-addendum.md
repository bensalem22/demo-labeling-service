## Department constitution gate

- [ ] The spec has approved, baseline-pinned requirements and measurable acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Requirement-to-test traceability includes negative and boundary cases.
- [ ] Data handling and least-privilege constraints have an accountable reviewer.
- [ ] Architecture choices and assurance assumptions have human owners.
- [ ] A sanitized session decision record and learning review are in the delivery tasks.
- [ ] Conflicts and exception requests are resolved, not silently overridden.

If any required item is unmet, mark affected implementation tasks BLOCKED.
